# Audit of PyGCGC on the September 2026 runs

24 September 2026. Every number here is printed by a script in
[`benchmarks/real_data_audit/`](../../benchmarks/real_data_audit/); outputs are regenerated in
`real_data/audit_outputs/` by running them.

## Period and phase (`period_and_phase.py`)

`estimate_period` returned 8.0000 s on all four runs with high confidence (standard error 1.07 to
2.33 ms, implying at most 0.6 to 1.3 s of drift over the run, which is why anchors remain the
check). The bleed-based phase put the cut at 2.55 to 2.69 s. With the internal standards now in
the target table, phase can come from anchors instead.

## ⛓️ n-Alkane ladder (`ladder.py`)

Independent check: at every carbon the ladder assigns up to C21, the 14 eV spectrum of the same
acquisition is read after local background subtraction; the n-alkane molecular ion (14n + 2)
should be the strongest of those of neighbouring carbons. Above C21 the molecular ion is out of
range, so only spacing can be judged. The spot checks by hand agreed: C13 to C18 and C20 on ATWC
carry their own molecular ion at 14 eV (1.4 to 3.3% of base) and at 70 eV (0.5 to 1.6%).

Seeds C15 and C16 (clicked a few seconds off), qualifier gate on (default) and off. "Confirmed"
means the carbon's own molecular ion is the strongest of those within three carbons at 14 eV.

| Run | Gate | Range | Carbons | Missing | Confirmed to C21 | Contradicted | No 14 eV evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATWC | on | C11–C29 | 18 | C28 | 8 | C12 | C11, C19 |
| ATWC | off | C10–C30 | 21 | none | 8 | C10, C12 | C11, C19 |
| BTWC | on | C11–C26 | 15 | C12 | 7 | none | C11, C18, C20 |
| BTWC | off | C9–C26 | 18 | none | 9 | C9 | C11, C18, C20 |
| T191 | on | C10–C30 | 20 | C26 | 10 | none | C19, C21 |
| T191 | off | C9–C30 | 22 | none | 11 | none | C19, C21 |
| T60 | on | C9–C29 | 20 | C28 | 11 | none | C16, C17 |
| T60 | off | C9–C30 | 22 | none | 11 | none | C16, C17 |

Propagating the ATWC ladder (as the ⛓️ panel's Propagate does) found 15, 16 and 17 of its 18
carbons on BTWC, T191 and T60, 14 to 15 of them within one modulation of each run's own ladder.
Comparing the four ladders with each other, ATWC puts C11 eight modulations early (887 s against
951 s on the diesel runs, where the 14 eV molecular ion confirms 951 s) and its default ladder
put C22 at 3319 s against 3367 s with the gate off.

Findings:

- The n-alkane qualifier ratios on this instrument tune are 71/57 = 0.55 to 0.66 and
  85/57 = 0.30 to 0.43, lower than the defaults (0.66, 0.44) measured on the earlier
  acquisition. With the default ±30% gate a few alkanes are rejected; with the gate off the
  ladders are longer and the extra carbons check out.
- Wrong assignments come from a branched alkane with alkane-like ratios close to where the next
  n-alkane is predicted (C11 on ATWC, C22 and C23 on the others). A spacing check against the
  neighbours was tried: at an 8 s modulation, positions quantised to one modulation make
  neighbouring spacings uncertain by 5 to 10%, so a check tight enough to reject these also
  rejected true alkanes on T60, and one loose enough to keep them changed nothing. It was not
  kept.
- A ladder grown from two seeds typed in the wrong carbon order (for example a C15 click that
  was really C21) ran backwards through the run with no warning. `find_alkane_series` now
  refuses seeds that do not elute in carbon order or that resolve to the same peak.

## 🫧 Siloxane (`siloxane.py`)

The remover scores the legacy 🔍 blobs. Independent labels from each blob's own spectrum:
"siloxane" if at least half its volume sits on characteristic Si fragments (73, 96, 133, 147,
191, 193, 207 to 209, 221, 267, 281 to 283) with one of 73, 147, 207, 221, 267 or 281 as base
peak; "clearly not" if under 10% does.

| ATWC | 5,929 | 85% | 38 | 4,830 |
| T60 | 5,316 | 84% | 21 | 4,337 |

Siloxane-like blobs caught (and clearly non-siloxane blobs wrongly caught) by threshold:

| Per-pixel fit | T60 | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.8% | 60.7% | 2.4% |

Findings:

- Legacy footprints cover 84 to 85% of the image on these runs, so a blob flagged for bleed
  usually also holds hydrocarbons. At the default settings ATWC loses 4.5% of all its m/z 57
  signal and 1.9% of m/z 91 to blocking, for 6 of 38 siloxane peaks caught.
- The two measured references are complementary: ATWC's siloxane peaks are mostly cyclic
  (73/281), T60's bleed-like (207). Scoring against both and keeping the larger fixes the misses.
- The per-pixel fit removes most of the bleed on m/z 207 with no loss on 57, 91 or 128, but
  hardly touches the cyclic ions with the bleed reference.

## 🏷️ Internal standards (`internal_standards.py`)

Located on each run from its characteristic ions, then labelled once on ATWC and matched on the
others by `core.istd.match_run`:

| Standard | Quantifier | t1 (min) | t2 (s) | Match on BTWC, T191, T60 | Δt1 (s) | Volume CV over 4 runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| acenaphthene-d10 | 164 | 33.5 | 3.74 | high, high, high | −1.0 to +1.2 | 21% |
| phenanthrene-d10 | 188 | 45.1 | 4.60 | high, high, high | +0.9 to +1.5 | 19% |
| n-tetracosane-d50 | 66 or 82 | 60.4 | 1.33 | high, high, high | −0.2 to +0.9 | 17% |
| chrysene-d12 | 240 | 65.6 | 6.34 | high, high, high | +0.3 to +0.6 | 23% |
| perylene-d12 | 264 | 76.0 | 9.0 to 9.1 (wraps) | high, high, high | −1.4 to −0.8 | 32% |

Combined scores 0.93 to 0.99, spectral similarity 0.97 to 0.99. 🧪 found all five in all four runs
at signal-to-noise 6×10⁴ to 1.4×10⁵ with no flags (after the fix below). The volume spread of 17
to 32% between runs is the injection and matrix variation the standards exist to correct; it is
within the 50% flag limit, so no run was flagged. For n-tetracosane-d50 the
intensity × m/z ranking picks m/z 82 over the base peak 66; either is specific to deuterated
alkanes.

Perylene-d12 elutes at the end of the run with a true t2 of about 9.1 s, one period above the
image; 🧪 found it in every run but always flagged it "off position", because it is recorded in
the next modulation at t2 about 1.1 s. Fixed: candidates are now compared and reported on the side
of the wrap nearest the table's t2. `internal_standards.py` writes a draft target table of the
five standards to `real_data/target_table_istd.csv`.

## 🧪 Quantification (`quant_semisynthetic.py`)

A semi-synthetic sequence: the real runs, cropped to 12 to 52 min, with two internal standards
and four targets written in at scan level with counting noise, five calibration levels (1 to
20 ng), three samples of known amount and a blank, a random injection factor of 0.8 to 1.2 per
run and a small retention drift. Two targets use ions the matrix hardly carries (m/z 136, 212),
two use busy ones (m/z 57 beside the alkanes, m/z 105 among the alkylbenzenes). The target table
gives positions off by a few seconds and leaves qualifier ratios blank. `pygcgc quant` then runs
exactly as on a real sequence.

| Target | Quantifier | Median calibration error | Curve passes | Sample errors (S1, S2, S3) | Errors above 20% left unflagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T_unique136 | 136 | 3.2% | yes | +7.3%, −2.1%, +27.1% (0.4 ng, below L1, flagged) | none |
| T_unique212 | 212 | 0.1% | yes | −0.3%, 0.0%, −0.1% | none |
| T_branched57 | 57 | 11.9% | no (worst 36%) | +46% (flagged), −15%, +41% | CAL_L4 +36%, CAL_L5 −22%, S3 +41% |
| T_arom105 | 105 | 28.6% | no (worst 54%) | −24%, +64%, +9% (all flagged) | CAL_L5 −38% |

The blank read 0.3 to 0.6 ng equivalents for the three targets whose position the matrix
occupies, all flagged; the fourth was not found. 38 of 54 results were "ok", 15 "check".

Findings:

- The chain works: internal standards found in all nine runs, their volumes tracking the
  injection factor, which the response ratio cancels; positions learned from rough ones.
- On selective ions calibrations pass and samples are within a few per cent; a sample below the
  lowest level (0.4 ng) is 27% high and correctly flagged below LOQ.
- On busy ions the co-eluting matrix adds to the peak, 15 to 60% either way, and both curves
  fail their back-calculation limits (reported as such). This is a property of the ion choice,
  not a bug.
- Before the fix, blank `qual_ratios` meant no qualifier was checked at all, contrary to the
  README. They are now learned from the calibration runs; the m/z 105 interference is then
  flagged in most runs. Interference by a compound with a similar spectrum (a branched alkane on
  m/z 57) passes the qualifier check, as it must.

## 🗂️ Batch

`pygcgc batch <semi-synthetic folder> --workers 3 --siloxane --cluster --report all` prepared nine
runs, quantified them, built a 3,286-feature table, clustered 26,461 peaks into 163 clusters
(1.1% of volume unassigned) and wrote `report.xlsx`, `.html` and `.pdf` in 409 s.

## Code and repository

- The previously uncommitted work (MS-aware components, 🧬, 🧩, 🏷️ internal standards, autosave,
  and their tests) was committed as found before anything changed.
- No duplicate function definitions remain (all modules checked); one unused public function
  (`components.fit_candidate_region`); three files carried a byte-order mark, now removed.
- GUI: Go on step 11 raised `KeyError`; step 11 was missing from the toolbar; a disabled
  "3D, arrives in milestone 8" tab was left over. Fixed.
- Two tests were stale (a scan count of the old acquisition, and the step order before step 11).
- Documentation contradicted the code in places: the period estimator was described as removed
  while the Fold step uses it; bracketing between calibration sets was "not built" while it is
  the default; several settings dialogs, the figure button, the homologous-series button and
  stencil editing were listed as missing though present; the internal-standard step was not
  documented. Corrected.
