Compound-class assignment of 4068 blobs from the untreated 70 eV run (Repsol3 test3, 1 uL injection, m/z 38 to 300, modulation period 10 s).

(a) Blob apexes in the first- and second-dimension retention plane, coloured and shaped by chemical group. Marker area increases with blob volume (logarithmic, m/z summed over the footprint). (b) Distribution of the second-dimension retention time above the n-alkane line (t2 minus the t2 of the C12 to C28 ladder at the blob's own carbon number) for each class with at least five blobs; the number of blobs is given in brackets. Boxes show the interquartile range, the bar the median and whiskers the 10th and 90th percentiles. The dotted line marks the alkane line. Blobs earlier than 11 min (solvent front) and blobs outside the ladder's carbon range are omitted from (b).

Classes were assigned from each blob's apex mass spectrum (footprint spectrum where the apex spectrum was empty, 319 blobs) with the rules in chemical_classes.toml. The alkane ladder was found from two seeds (C13 and C18). The t2 offset enters the rules only as supporting evidence, which can raise a class score from 0.5 to 1.0; no class requires it, so panel (b) is a consistency check and not an independent measurement. The alkane line is the ladder itself, so the alkane median lies at zero by construction only if the alkane blobs sit on the ladder. The dense block of blobs at t2 above 4 s and t1 below 20 min in (a) is not separated from the rest; it produces the upper whisker of the alkylbenzene box in (b). The S-heteroaromatic class was disabled. Blobs that met no class at a score of 0.3 are shown as unclassified.
